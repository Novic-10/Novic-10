Executive Summary & Overview

This writeup details the analysis of AWS S3 Access logs and CloudTrail data to investigate a suspected data exfiltration incident. The IT team identified a critical misconfiguration where an overly permissive policy was applied to an AWS user account, potentially leading to unauthorized access and data leakage from S3 buckets.

Our investigation focused on identifying the user responsible, the specific policy applied, the administrator who made the change, the affected S3 buckets, and any subsequent data retrieval or exfiltration activities. We leveraged command-line tools like cat and grep to parse the provided cloudtrail.json logs.

Key findings include the identification of user CodyShaddock receiving the GrantS3FullAccess policy from TimSmithADMIN, subsequent enumeration and bulk retrieval of sensitive files from lab-bucket-sensitive, and an attempt to cover tracks by removing the policy.

Key Takeaways & Security Insights

  • •
    Principle of Least Privilege: The incident highlights the critical importance of adhering to the principle of least privilege. Granting GrantS3FullAccess instead of a specific, scoped policy directly led to the potential for data exfiltration.
  • •
    CloudTrail Monitoring: Continuous monitoring and alerting on AWS CloudTrail logs are essential. Rapid detection of events like AttachUserPolicy, ListBucket, GetObject, and DeleteUserPolicy can significantly reduce detection and response times.
  • •
    Insider Threat Detection (MITRE ATT&CK T1098.003): The scenario demonstrates a privilege escalation attempt where CodyShaddock received excessive permissions from TimSmithADMIN. Organizations must implement robust access review processes and anomaly detection for administrative actions.
  • •
    Data Exfiltration Detection (MITRE ATT&CK T1530): The download of sensitive files (project-orion-CONFIDENTIAL.zip, project-chimera-CONFIDENTIAL.zip) from S3 buckets highlights the need for data loss prevention (DLP) strategies and monitoring for unusual data transfer volumes or access patterns.
  • •
    Anti-Forensics Awareness (MITRE ATT&CK T1070): The attempt to remove the GrantS3FullAccess policy using DeleteUserPolicy is a clear anti-forensics tactic. Security teams should be aware of such actions and ensure logs are immutable and centrally managed to prevent tampering.
