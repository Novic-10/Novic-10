Executive Summary & Overview

This challenge involved decoding a highly obfuscated string found on the personal device of a captured threat actor, John Galkovich Tucker, believed to be part of the ShadowCipher group. The file, named JGT’sSecretSauce.txt, contained a series of numbers that required multi-stage decoding to reveal critical intelligence about the group's operations and members.

The investigation focused on identifying the encoding formats and applying the correct decryption methods to uncover details about external partners, key personnel, and future operations of the ShadowCipher group.

Question & Answer Solutions

1. What is first type of encoding format used on the content of the text file?

Decimal

Reasoning: Initial analysis of the file content revealed a series of space-delimited numbers, indicating a decimal encoding. This was the first step in the decoding process.

2. What is the second type of data format used on the content of the text file?

base64

Reasoning: After decoding from decimal, the resulting string appeared to be Base64 encoded. Applying the "From Base64" recipe in CyberChef confirmed this.

3. Almost there, looks like they’ve run a some type of shift cipher against the text. Someone said 11 doesn’t work? If its encoded one way, you would have to decode it the other way right? What's the rotation amount needed to decode this message?

ROT15

Reasoning: The hint about a shift cipher and the CTF title "Caesar Salad" suggested a ROT cipher. Experimentation with different rotation amounts, specifically considering the inverse of a common ROT value, revealed that ROT15 successfully decoded the message.

4. Looks like there are 3 external partners involved with this mission. Mogo Inc. is a manufacturer of steel. Who does Mogo Inc. belong to?

Theodore Mogo

Reasoning: The fully decoded text explicitly mentioned "Mogo Inc." and identified its owner as Theodore Mogo.

5. There's a brother and sister involved with running areas of ShadowCipher. What are their names?

Carlos Trottleburger, Francine Trottleburger

Reasoning: The decoded message revealed the names of the brother and sister involved in ShadowCipher's operations.

6. What is the name of ShadowCipers next operation?

Operation Sharpener

Reasoning: The decrypted intelligence clearly stated the name of the next ShadowCipher operation.

7. John Galkovich Tucker isn’t the head of ShadowCipher? What is he then?

Decoy

Reasoning: The decoded message explicitly stated that John Galkovich Tucker was merely a decoy, not the true leader of ShadowCipher.

8. Where is the operation going to take place?

New York

Reasoning: The decrypted information specified New York as the location for the upcoming operation.

Key Takeaways & Security Insights

  • •
    Multi-Stage Obfuscation: Threat actors often employ multiple layers of encoding to protect sensitive information. This challenge demonstrated a three-stage process involving Decimal (Space Delimiter), Base64, and a ROT15 cipher.
  • •
    Importance of Contextual Clues: The CTF title "Caesar Salad" was a crucial hint for identifying the final ROT cipher, highlighting how seemingly minor details can guide decryption efforts.
  • •
    CyberChef Proficiency: Tools like CyberChef are indispensable for quickly identifying and applying various encoding/decoding recipes, streamlining the analysis of obfuscated data.
  • •
    Intelligence Gathering: Decoding such files can yield significant intelligence, including the identities of key individuals, organizational structures, and future operational plans, which are vital for law enforcement and counter-terrorism efforts.
